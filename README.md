# 📌 Retail Sales & Customer Analytics (Tableau)

## 📖 Overview  
This project analyzes retail sales and customer behavior using Tableau. The dashboards provide insights into revenue trends, profitability, and customer purchasing patterns over time.

## 🎯 Objective  
To build interactive dashboards that help understand sales performance, product profitability, and customer behavior for better business decision-making.

## 📊 What I Did  
- Designed two interactive Tableau dashboards:  
  - **Sales Dashboard** to track revenue, profit, and sales trends.  
  - **Customer Dashboard** to analyze customer orders, frequency, and profitability.  
- Compared monthly sales and profit across two years to identify trends.  
- Analyzed product subcategories to identify profitable and loss-making items.  
- Studied customer purchase patterns and repeat buying behavior.

## 🛠️ Tools Used  
- Tableau  

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- A small group of customers contributed to the majority of total profit (Pareto pattern).  
- Certain product subcategories consistently generated losses.  
- Sales showed clear seasonal patterns across the year.  
- Most customers placed only 1–3 orders, indicating low repeat purchase rates.
