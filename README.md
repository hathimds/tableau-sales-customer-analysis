# Tableau Sales & Customer Performance Analysis

This project analyzes retail sales and customer behavior using Tableau dashboards. The objective is to uncover trends in sales, profit, customer distribution, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

## Project Overview
The analysis focuses on:
- Sales performance trends over time  
- Customer behavior and order distribution  
- Product category and subcategory performance  
- Key business KPIs such as total sales, profit, quantity, and customer metrics  

Interactive dashboards were designed to provide actionable insights for business stakeholders.

## Dashboards
### Sales Dashboard
- Total Sales, Profit, and Quantity KPIs  
- Sales and profit trends over time  
- Subcategory performance comparison  

### Customer Dashboard
- Total customers and orders  
- Sales per customer trends  
- Customer distribution by number of orders  
- Top customers by profit  

## Dataset
The project uses a structured retail dataset with:
- Fact table: Orders and transactional data  
- Dimension tables: Customers, Products, and Locations  

## Tools & Techniques
- Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public  
- Data cleaning and transformation  
- Calculated fields and KPIs  
- Time series analysis  
- Customer segmentation and ranking  

## Deliverables
- Interactive Tableau dashboards  
- Analytical report (PDF)  
- Tableau workbook (.twbx)  
- Project documentation and datasets  

## Key Insights
- Sales and profit show seasonal variation across weeks  
- Certain subcategories drive the majority of revenue  
- A small group of customers contributes disproportionately to profit  
- Customer order frequency varies significantly across segments  

## Strategic Recommendations
- Focus marketing on high-value customers  
- Optimize inventory for top-performing subcategories  
- Target low-frequency customers with retention campaigns  
- Monitor weekly sales trends for demand planning
